OFFLINKA review sample
======================

This folder stands in for the Offlinka USB-C flash drive (sold on offlinka.cz).
The real drive holds the same kind of files, only more of them (about 35 GB).

How to use it with the Offlinka app:
1. Download the zip into Download (Android) or Downloads (iPhone/iPad) and unzip
   it there (Android: Files by Google; iPhone/iPad: tap it in the Files app).
   This creates the folder OFFLINKA, which contains Knihovna and Telefon.
2. Open the Offlinka app and tap "Vybrat flashku" ("Choose drive").
   Android: tap "Vybrat znovu" ("Choose again") if the drive is not found.
3. Open Download(s), pick the folder OFFLINKA, then confirm
   (Android: "Use this folder" and "Allow"; iPhone/iPad: "Open").
   The app finds the books inside and reads them in place, fully offline.

What is inside:
- Telefon/wikipedia_cs_all_mini-...zim   Czech Wikipedia, phone edition (+ .offidx search index)
- Telefon/offlinka_mul_hry.zim           10 games: chess, draughts, five in a row, battleships,
                                         sudoku, minesweeper, solitaire, memory, 2048, Hextris
- Knihovna/maps_en_slovakia_2026-06.zim  offline map of Slovakia
The emergency guides are built into the app and work without any drive.

Try: search "cesky krumlov" (no accents needed), open an article, the map, the games.
