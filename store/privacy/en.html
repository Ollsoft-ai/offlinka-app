Offlinka app privacy policy

Version 1.1, effective 22 November 2026. Applies to the Offlinka app for Android and iOS.

The app collects no data. It never connects to the internet and itself sends nothing to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting of its own.

1. Who publishes the app

1.1 The Offlinka app is published by Ollsoft s.r.o., company ID (IČO) 19336039, Příčná 1892/4, Nové Město, 110 00 Prague 1, Czech Republic.

1.2 Contact, including for data protection: info@offlinka.cz.

1.3 We process data under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). We have not appointed a data protection officer, as we are not required to.

2. What the app does not do

2.1 The app has no internet access. On Android it does not have the internet permission; on iOS it makes no network connections. It does not open links in articles that lead to the internet. The one exception is the link to this policy in About: it opens your web browser, which is a separate app. Phone numbers, email addresses and SMS links in the texts open the Phone, email or Messages app only after you tap them.

2.2 It collects no personal data and no usage data. It has no accounts, ads, measurement or analytics tools, sends no crash reports itself, and does not use your location, camera, microphone or contacts. On Android it also turns off the usage statistics of the system WebView component that shows the pages from the drive, and Google Safe Browsing; all content comes from the drive.

2.3 We sell and share nothing, because we receive nothing.

3. What the app stores only on your device

3.1 To work, the app keeps the following on your phone or tablet:

  • settings: language, light or dark mode, text size,
  • saved articles, reading history and recent searches,
  • search indexes of the books, which it builds itself from the content of the drive,
  • the permission to read the drive you picked, so it does not have to ask again,
  • data that pages from the drive store, such as game scores and the last map view,
  • a copy of the emergency guides from the app's installation package,
  • a temporary copy of a file you open from the drive in another app (a PDF, for example); the next file you open replaces it.

3.2 This data stays on your device. We have no access to it.

3.3 The app only reads the drive. It writes nothing to it and deletes nothing from it. It copies a file only when you open that file from the drive in another app, and only temporarily, in its own storage (point 3.1).

3.4 On Android the app has backup switched off, so nothing from it goes into a Google backup. On iPhone, if iCloud Backup is on, the system may include the settings, saved articles and history in a backup in your account. Apple manages that backup under its own terms. We have no access to it.

4. How to delete it

  • Delete the history in the app: Saved → History → Clear history.
  • Remove a saved article from the Saved list.
  • Delete recent searches in Settings → Clear recent searches.
  • Revoke the permission to read the drive in Settings → Forget this drive.
  • Delete everything at once by uninstalling the app. On Android also in the phone's Settings → Apps → Offlinka → Storage → Clear data.

5. When you write to us

5.1 If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply and to deal with your request. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep messages for at most 2 years.

5.2 You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing and to object. Write to info@offlinka.cz. We reply within one month at the latest.

5.3 You can complain to the Czech data protection authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, uoou.gov.cz, or to the authority in your EU country.

6. App stores

6.1 You download the app from Google Play or the App Store. Google or Apple process download and purchase data under their own policies; we receive aggregate statistics from them, without your personal data. If you allowed sharing diagnostics on your phone, Google or Apple also show us crash reports: a technical record of the error, the device model and the system version, without your name or what you were reading.

7. Changes

7.1 The current version is always on this page. If the app ever were to start collecting anything, we would change this policy and the app's store listings first.
