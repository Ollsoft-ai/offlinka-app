Offlinka opens the Offlinka USB-C drive right on your phone. Czech and Slovak Wikipedia with pictures, maps of Czechia and Slovakia, first aid, books and games. Everything works without the internet: at the cottage, in the mountains, when travelling or when the network is down.

What is inside:
• The complete Czech and Slovak Wikipedia with pictures.
• The one million most-read articles of English Wikipedia (text only) and the English medical articles.
• Maps of Czechia and Slovakia, down to every street.
• Emergency guides in Czech: first aid and power cuts. They are built into the app, so they work even without the drive.
• Czech Wikibooks, Wikiquote, Wikisource, Wikiversity and Wiktionary, and the English Wikivoyage guide to Europe.
• The games 2048 and draughts.

How it works:
• Plug the drive into the USB-C port and pick it once. Next time, just plug it in.
• The app reads the books straight from the drive. Nothing is copied to your phone and nothing on the drive changes.
• One search covers every book. You can type without accents. The first time, search needs a little while to get ready.
• Save articles, and find what you read in your history.
• Choose the text size and dark mode.

Privacy:
The app has no internet access and sends nothing. It collects no data and has no accounts or ads.

What you need:
• An Offlinka drive, sold at offlinka.cz.
• iPhone 15 or later, or an iPad with a USB-C port, running iOS 17 or later. Older iPhones with a Lightning port do not work.
