Offlinka reads the Offlinka USB-C drive right on your phone: Czech and Slovak Wikipedia with pictures, English Wikipedia, maps of Czechia and Slovakia, emergency guides and games. Everything works without the internet.

Plug the drive into the USB-C port and select it once. The app remembers it. Nothing is copied to the phone and nothing on the drive changes.

Search works without accents. Save articles and find them again in the history. Set the text size and dark mode in the app.

The app has no internet access, collects no data, and has no accounts or ads.

You need an Offlinka drive (offlinka.cz) and an iPhone or iPad with a USB-C port.
