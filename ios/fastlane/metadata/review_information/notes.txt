Offlinka is the free companion app of the Offlinka flash drive, a 64 GB USB-C drive we sell in Czechia and Slovakia (https://offlinka.cz). The drive holds Czech and Slovak Wikipedia with pictures, English Wikipedia, maps of Czechia and Slovakia, emergency guides and 10 small offline games (chess, draughts, five in a row, battleships, sudoku, minesweeper, solitaire, memory, 2048 and Hextris), as ZIM files. The app reads these files in place from the drive the user picks once in the Files folder picker (security-scoped bookmark). It never writes to the drive.

The app makes no network connections: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases. Links to the internet inside articles are not opened. The only links out open Safari: the privacy policy in Settings > About, and on the first screen "Nemáte ještě Offlinku? Kupte ji zde" ("Don't have Offlinka yet? Buy one here"), which opens our shop for the physical drive (offlinka.cz, offlinka.sk for Slovak).

HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT THE DRIVE
1. Built-in content: the emergency guides (first aid, power cuts; in Czech) are bundled in the app. On the first screen tap "Otevřít první pomoc" ("Open first aid" when the device language is English).
2. Sample library (1.5 GB; about 3 GB free space needed to unzip it): https://github.com/Ollsoft-ai/offlinka-app/releases/download/review-sample-2026-11/offlinka-review-sample.zip. Download it in Safari; it lands in Files under On My iPhone > Downloads, or iCloud Drive > Downloads (Safari's default). Tap the zip in the Files app to unzip it there. This creates the folder "OFFLINKA", which contains the folders "Knihovna" and "Telefon". Then in Offlinka tap "Vybrat flashku" ("Choose drive" in English), open that Downloads folder, choose the folder "OFFLINKA" and tap Open.
The sample is trimmed: Czech Wikipedia phone edition (text only, no pictures), the map of Slovakia and the 10 games; the emergency guides are built into the app. The real drive (35 GB, 14 books) adds Czech and Slovak Wikipedia with pictures, English Wikipedia, the map of Czechia and the other books in the screenshots. Try: search "cesky krumlov" (no accents needed), Home > "Prohlédněte si mapu" ("Explore the map"), Home > "Zahrajte si 2048" ("Play 2048").
The games are HTML5 pages inside the drive's ZIM file, shown in WKWebView with no native bridge, offline, with no purchases.

Demo video: the attached video (1:39) shows the app in the iOS Simulator with the same files as on the drive: choosing the drive, searching without accents, an article, the offline map, a game and the built-in first-aid guides. On an iPhone the drive is plugged in and picked once in the Files picker.
4. Second video (1:22, offlinka-iphone-airplane-mode.mp4): the same build on a real iPhone in Airplane Mode with the real Offlinka drive connected: Home, the article Sněžka with find in page, the games, the offline map of Prague and the library with all 14 books.

Requirements: iPhone 15 or later, or an iPad with USB-C, iOS 17+. Lightning iPhones cannot connect the drive.
