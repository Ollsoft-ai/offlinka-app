Offlinka is the free companion app of the Offlinka flash drive, a 64 GB USB-C drive we sell in Czechia and Slovakia (https://offlinka.cz). The drive holds Czech and Slovak Wikipedia with pictures, English Wikipedia, maps of Czechia and Slovakia, emergency guides and two small games (2048 and draughts), as ZIM files. The app reads these files in place from the drive the user picks once in the Files folder picker (security-scoped bookmark). It never writes to the drive.

The app has no network access at all: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases. Links to the internet inside articles are not opened.

HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT THE DRIVE
1. Built-in content: the emergency guides (first aid, power cuts; in Czech) are bundled in the app. On the first screen tap "Otevřít první pomoc" ("Open first aid" when the device language is English).
2. Sample library: we provide the same files as on the drive at [SAMPLE URL]. In Safari download the zip, open it in the Files app so it unpacks into a folder "OFFLINKA", then in Offlinka tap "Vybrat flashku" ("Choose drive" in English), choose that folder and tap Open. The app then shows the library as with the real drive: Czech Wikipedia (phone edition), the emergency guides and the games.
3. Demo video: the attached video shows an iPhone 15 with the real drive: picking the drive, searching without accents, reading an article, the offline map, unplugging and plugging the drive back.
We are happy to send a physical drive to App Review on request.

Requirements: iPhone 15 or later, or an iPad with USB-C, iOS 17+. Lightning iPhones cannot connect the drive.
